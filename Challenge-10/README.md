Challenge Objective
The university wants to understand environmental conditions across its campus and identify areas that may need attention. Develop a
menu-driven C program that analyses seven days of temperature, humidity, and Air Quality Index (AQI) readings from five campus
zones. Your program must turn the readings into a clear risk report, a Green Index ranking, and practical recommendations

Algorithm: ECOGUARD Campus Environment Monitoring System

1. Start the program.
2. Declare arrays for temperature, humidity and AQI for five zones and seven days.
3. Display the program menu.
4. Accept environmental readings from the user.
5. Validate the readings and reject invalid values.
6. Display the temperature, humidity and AQI matrices.
7. Calculate the seven-day averages for each zone.
8. Detect risk days using temperature, humidity and AQI thresholds.
9. Calculate the Green Index for each zone and limit negative component scores to zero.
10. Rank the zones according to their Green Index, assigning equal ranks to equal scores.
11. Display eco-friendly recommendations based on the readings.
12. Repeat the menu until the user selects Exit.
13. Stop.
